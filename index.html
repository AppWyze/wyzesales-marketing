Customer segmentation

Know who to thank, who to call, and who you are about to lose

WyzeSales scores every customer on how recently they bought, how often, and how much they spend, ranked against your own customer base. Then it sorts them into ten plain-English segments, each with a suggestion on what to do.

Best Growing Cooling At risk Dormant
  • Best customers, Loyal regulars, Rising and Warming up, so you can nurture them
  • Cooling off, Fading, Slipping away and Win back now, so you can act before they are gone
  • Share of customers against share of sales, for every segment
  • Click a segment to see exactly who is in it, then jump straight into their sales
